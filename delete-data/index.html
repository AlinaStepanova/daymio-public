Daymio: Private Period Tracker, by Alina Stepanova

1. Everything you record in the app

Your period days, flow, symptoms and notes are stored only on your device. We never receive a copy, so you delete them yourself, at any time:

  1. Open Daymio and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete all data and confirm with Delete everything.

Uninstalling the app also deletes all of this data. It is deleted immediately and permanently; nothing is kept, and we cannot restore it.

2. Your purchase record

If you bought Daymio Premium, our purchase service RevenueCat holds an anonymous record so the app can recognise your purchase. It contains an anonymous app user ID, your Daymio purchase history, your device model, operating-system and app version, and your IP address. It never contains your name, e-mail address or anything you record in the app.

To have this record deleted:

  1. Send an e-mail to alina.stepanova.work@gmail.com with the subject “Daymio data deletion”.
  2. If you can, include the order number of your purchase. It is the only way we can find your anonymous record:
    • Google Play: in the receipt e-mail from Google Play, or in your Google Play order history. It starts with “GPA.”.
    • App Store: in the receipt e-mail from Apple, or at reportaproblem.apple.com, listed as “Order ID”.

We delete the record within one month and confirm by e-mail.

What is kept: Google Play or the App Store keeps its own record of your purchase under its own terms; we cannot delete that. Your purchase itself stays valid. After the deletion, Premium may appear locked in the app; tap Restore Purchase in Settings to unlock it again. This creates a new anonymous record from the store's data.
